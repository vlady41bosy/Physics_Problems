## 9. Momentum Comparison Solutions

To determine which object has greater momentum, we calculate the momentum for each using the formula $p = mv$, where $m$ is the mass and $v$ is the velocity. To ensure proper SI units, we first convert the mass from grams to kilograms.

* **Calculate the momentum of the fly:**
    Mass of the fly: $m_f = 2 \text{ g} = 0.002 \text{ kg}$
    Velocity of the fly: $v_f = 10 \text{ m/s}$
    $$p_f = m_f v_f$$
    $$p_f = (0.002)(10) = 0.02 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

* **Calculate the momentum of the tennis ball:**
    Mass of the ball: $m_b = 60 \text{ g} = 0.060 \text{ kg}$
    Velocity of the ball: $v_b = 1 \text{ m/s}$
    $$p_b = m_b v_b$$
    $$p_b = (0.060)(1) = 0.06 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

* **Compare the results:**
    Comparing the two values:
    $$0.06 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s} > 0.02 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$
    Therefore, the **tennis ball has greater momentum**, even though it is moving significantly slower than the fly. Its much larger mass makes up for the difference in speed.
