### **Step-by-Step Solution**

**1. Identify the Given Information**
* Total time for the echo to be heard ($t_{total}$): $1 \text{ s}$
* Speed of sound in air ($v$): $343 \text{ m/s}$

**2. State the Relevant Formula**
The basic formula for distance is:

$$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$$

However, in an echo scenario, the sound travels *to* the cliff and then reflects *back* to the person. This means the total distance the sound wave travels is twice the actual distance to the cliff ($2d$). 

So, the formula becomes:

$$2d = v \cdot t_{total}$$

To find the one-way distance to the cliff ($d$), we rearrange the formula to divide the total distance by 2:

$$d = \frac{v \cdot t_{total}}{2}$$

**3. Calculate the Distance**
Substitute the given values into the rearranged formula:

$$d = \frac{343 \cdot 1}{2}$$

$$d = \frac{343}{2}$$

$$d = 171.5 \text{ m}$$

**Answer:** The cliff is **171.5 meters** away.
