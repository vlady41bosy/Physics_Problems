

### **Step-by-Step Solution**

**1. Compare with the Standard Wave Equation**
The standard mathematical form of a traveling wave is:

$$y(x,t) = A\sin(kx - \omega t)$$

Where:
* **$A$** is the amplitude.
* **$k$** is the wave number ($k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$).
* **$\omega$** is the angular frequency ($\omega = 2\pi f$).

Given your equation:

$$y(x,t) = 0.05\sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)$$

By comparing the two equations, we can extract the key parameters:
* $A = 0.05$

* $k = 2\pi$

* $\omega = 50\pi$

**2. Solve for (a) Amplitude $A$**
The amplitude is directly given by the coefficient in front of the sine function. Since $y$ is in meters:

$$A = 0.05 \text{ m}$$

**3. Solve for (b) Wavelength $\lambda$**
Use the relationship between the wave number ($k$) and wavelength ($\lambda$):

$$k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$$

Substitute $k = 2\pi$ into the equation:

$$2\pi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$$

$$\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{2\pi}$$

$$\lambda = 1 \text{ m}$$

**4. Solve for (c) Frequency $f$**
Use the relationship between angular frequency ($\omega$) and standard frequency ($f$):

$$\omega = 2\pi f$$

Substitute $\omega = 50\pi$ into the equation:

$$50\pi = 2\pi f$$

$$f = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi}$$

$$f = 25 \text{ Hz}$$

**5. Solve for (d) Wave Speed $v$**
The speed of the wave can be calculated by multiplying the frequency by the wavelength ($v = f \cdot \lambda$), or by dividing the angular frequency by the wave number ($v = \frac{\omega}{k}$). 

Using $v = f \cdot \lambda$:

$$v = 25 \cdot 1$$

$$v = 25 \text{ m/s}$$

**Final Answers:**
* **a) Amplitude $A$:** $0.05 \text{ m}$
* **b) Wavelength $\lambda$:** $1 \text{ m}$
* **c) Frequency $f$:** $25 \text{ Hz}$
* **d) Wave speed $v$:** $25 \text{ m/s}$
