### **Step-by-Step Solution**

**1. Identify the Given Information**
* Distance separation (path difference), denoted as $\Delta x$: $\frac{\lambda}{3}$

**2. State the Relevant Formula**
A full wavelength ($\lambda$) corresponds to a full cycle of a wave, which is $2\pi$ radians. The phase difference ($\Delta \phi$) between two points is proportional to their physical distance separation ($\Delta x$). 

The formula connecting them is:

$$\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} \cdot \Delta x$$

**3. Calculate the Phase Difference**
Substitute the given path difference ($\Delta x = \frac{\lambda}{3}$) into the formula:

$$\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} \cdot \left(\frac{\lambda}{3}\right)$$

The $\lambda$ terms in the numerator and denominator cancel each other out:

$$\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{3}$$

**Answer:** The phase difference between the two points is **$\frac{2\pi}{3}$ radians**.
