

### **Step-by-Step Solution**

**1. Identify the Given Information**
* Frequency of the sound wave ($f$): $440 \text{ Hz}$
* Speed of sound in air ($v_{air}$): $343 \text{ m/s}$
* Speed of sound in water ($v_{water}$): $1482 \text{ m/s}$

**2. State the Relevant Formula**
The relationship between wave speed ($v$), frequency ($f$), and wavelength ($\lambda$) is governed by the wave equation:

$$v = f \cdot \lambda$$

To solve for wavelength, we rearrange the formula:

$$\lambda = \frac{v}{f}$$

**3. Calculate the Wavelength in Air**
Substitute the values for air into the rearranged formula:

$$\lambda_{air} = \frac{343}{440}$$

$$\lambda_{air} \approx 0.78 \text{ m}$$

**4. Calculate the Wavelength in Water**
Substitute the values for water into the formula:

$$\lambda_{water} = \frac{1482}{440}$$

$$\lambda_{water} \approx 3.37 \text{ m}$$

**Answer:** The wavelength of the sound wave is approximately **0.78 meters** in air and **3.37 meters** in water.
