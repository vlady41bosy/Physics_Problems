

## 5. Trigonometry: Vector Components (Step-by-Step)

**The Problem:**
A vector $\vec{A}$ has a magnitude of **15** and an angle $\theta = 60^\circ$ from the horizontal. Find its components.

### Step 1: Understand the Geometry

Imagine the vector as the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle:

* **Hypotenuse ($h$):** 15 (The total "strength" of the vector).
* **Horizontal Component ($A_x$):** The base of the triangle (adjacent to the angle).
* **Vertical Component ($A_y$):** The height of the triangle (opposite the angle).

### Step 2: Calculate the Horizontal Component ($A_x$)

To find the side **adjacent** to the angle, we use the **Cosine** function ($\cos = \text{adjacent} / \text{hypotenuse}$):


$$A_x = A \cdot \cos(\theta)$$

$$A_x = 15 \cdot \cos(60^\circ)$$

Since $\cos(60^\circ) = 0.5$:


$$A_x = 15 \cdot 0.5 = \mathbf{7.5}$$

### Step 3: Calculate the Vertical Component ($A_y$)

To find the side **opposite** the angle, we use the **Sine** function ($\sin = \text{opposite} / \text{hypotenuse}$):


$$A_y = A \cdot \sin(\theta)$$

$$A_y = 15 \cdot \sin(60^\circ)$$

Using the exact value $\sin(60^\circ) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ (or approximately $0.866$):


$$A_y = 15 \cdot 0.866 \approx \mathbf{12.99}$$

---


**Summary of Results:**

* **Horizontal ($A_x$):** 7.5 units
* **Vertical ($A_y$):** 12.99 units
