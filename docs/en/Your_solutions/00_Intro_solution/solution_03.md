
## 3. Proportionality (Step-by-Step)

**The Formula:**


$$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$

* $F$: Gravitational Force
* $G$: Gravitational Constant (this never changes)
* $m_1, m_2$: The masses of the two objects
* $r$: The distance between them

### Step 1: Identify the Changes

The problem gives us three specific changes to apply to our variables:

1. **Mass 1 is halved:** $m_1 \to \frac{1}{2}m_1$
2. **Mass 2 is halved:** $m_2 \to \frac{1}{2}m_2$
3. **Distance is doubled:** $r \to 2r$

### Step 2: Set up the New Equation ($F_{new}$)

We substitute these new values into the original formula. It's helpful to use parentheses to keep the math clean:


$$F_{new} = G \frac{(\frac{1}{2}m_1)(\frac{1}{2}m_2)}{(2r)^2}$$

### Step 3: Simplify the Numerator

Multiply the two masses together:


$$\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$$


So the top part becomes: $\frac{1}{4} (G \cdot m_1 \cdot m_2)$

### Step 4: Simplify the Denominator

When you double the distance, you must square the **entire** value $(2r)$:


$$(2r)^2 = 4r^2$$

### Step 5: Combine and Compare

Now we put the new numerator and denominator together:


$$F_{new} = \frac{\frac{1}{4}}{4} \cdot \left( G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2} \right)$$

To simplify the fraction $\frac{1/4}{4}$, you multiply the bottom numbers:


$$\frac{1}{4} \div 4 = \frac{1}{16}$$

**Result:**


$$F_{new} = \frac{1}{16} F_{original}$$

> **Final Answer:** The force $F$ changes by a factor of **1/16** (it becomes 16 times weaker).
