The general formula for the magnitude of the Lorentz force on a charged particle moving through a magnetic field is:
$$F_L = qvB\sin(\theta)$$

*   **$v$** is the velocity (speed).
*   **$B$** is the magnetic field strength.
*   **$\theta$** is the angle between the velocity vector and the magnetic field vector.
