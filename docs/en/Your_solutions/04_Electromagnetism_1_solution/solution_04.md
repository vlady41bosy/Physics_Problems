**Answer:**
*   **Electric Force ($F_e$):** $8.2 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$
*   **Gravitational Force ($F_g$):** $3.6 \times 10^{-47} \text{ N}$
*   **Ratio ($F_e / F_g$):** $2.3 \times 10^{39}$

**Short Explanation:**
This problem demonstrates a fundamental truth of the universe: at the atomic and subatomic scale, the electric force is astronomically stronger than gravity. The ratio shows that the electric attraction keeping the electron in orbit is roughly $2.3$ undecillion ($10^{39}$) times stronger than the gravitational pull between the two particles. Because of this massive difference, physicists and chemists completely ignore gravity when studying atoms.

Here is the step-by-step mathematical solution:

### **1. Identify the Constants**
To solve this, we need several universal constants and properties of an electron and a proton:
*   **Distance ($r$):** $5.3 \times 10^{-11} \text{ m}$
*   **Elementary charge ($e$):** $1.6 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}$ (The proton is $+e$ and the electron is $-e$)
*   **Coulomb's constant ($k$):** $8.99 \times 10^9 \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$
*   **Gravitational constant ($G$):** $6.67 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
*   **Mass of electron ($m_e$):** $9.11 \times 10^{-31} \text{ kg}$
*   **Mass of proton ($m_p$):** $1.67 \times 10^{-27} \text{ kg}$

### **2. Calculate the Electric Force ($F_e$)**
Use Coulomb's Law. We only need the magnitude, so we take the absolute value of the charges.
$$F_e = k \frac{|q_1 \cdot q_2|}{r^2}$$
$$F_e = (8.99 \times 10^9) \frac{(1.6 \times 10^{-19}) \cdot (1.6 \times 10^{-19})}{(5.3 \times 10^{-11})^2}$$
$$F_e = (8.99 \times 10^9) \frac{2.56 \times 10^{-38}}{2.809 \times 10^{-21}}$$
$$F_e \approx 8.2 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N}$$

### **3. Calculate the Gravitational Force ($F_g$)**
Use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation.
$$F_g = G \frac{m_e \cdot m_p}{r^2}$$
$$F_g = (6.67 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{(9.11 \times 10^{-31}) \cdot (1.67 \times 10^{-27})}{(5.3 \times 10^{-11})^2}$$
$$F_g = (6.67 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{1.52 \times 10^{-57}}{2.809 \times 10^{-21}}$$
$$F_g \approx 3.6 \times 10^{-47} \text{ N}$$

### **4. Calculate the Ratio**
Divide the electric force by the gravitational force to find out how many times stronger it is.
$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{F_e}{F_g}$$
$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{8.2 \times 10^{-8}}{3.6 \times 10^{-47}}$$
$$\text{Ratio} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}$$
