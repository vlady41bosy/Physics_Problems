

**Short Explanation:**
For an object to levitate, the forces pulling it down must perfectly balance the forces pushing it up. In this case, Earth's gravity pulls the proton downward, so we need an electric field to create an equal upward force. Because the proton has a positive charge, the electric field must point in the exact same direction as the desired force—straight up.

Here is the step-by-step mathematical solution:

### **1. Set Up the Equilibrium Condition**
For levitation (net force of zero), the upward Electric Force ($F_e$) must equal the downward Gravitational Force ($F_g$).

$$F_e = F_g$$

### **2. Write the Force Equations**
*   The electric force in a uniform electric field is $F_e = qE$ (where $q$ is the charge and $E$ is the electric field strength).
*   The gravitational force is $F_g = mg$ (where $m$ is the mass and $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity).

Substitute these into the equilibrium equation:

$$qE = mg$$

### **3. Solve for the Electric Field ($E$)**
Rearrange the formula to isolate $E$:

$$E = \frac{mg}{q}$$

### **4. Plug in the Values**
*   **$m$ (Mass):** $1.67 \times 10^{-27} \text{ kg}$
*   **$g$ (Gravity):** $9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$
*   **$q$ (Charge):** $1.6 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}$

$$E = \frac{(1.67 \times 10^{-27}) \cdot 9.8}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}$$

$$E = \frac{16.366 \times 10^{-27}}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}$$

To divide, subtract the exponent in the denominator from the exponent in the numerator ($-27 - (-19) = -8$):

$$E \approx 10.23 \times 10^{-8} \text{ N/C}$$

In proper scientific notation, this is:

**$$E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7} \text{ N/C}$$**
