Here is the step-by-step solution to find the number of turns in the secondary coil:

**Given:**

* Primary voltage ($V_p$) = $120\text{ V}$
* Secondary voltage ($V_s$) = $9.0\text{ V}$
* Primary turns ($N_p$) = $400$

**Formula:**
The relationship between voltage and the number of turns in an ideal transformer is given by the ratio:


$$\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p}$$

**Calculation:**
Rearranging the formula to solve for the number of secondary turns ($N_s$):


$$N_s = N_p \cdot \frac{V_s}{V_p}$$

Substitute the given values into the equation:


$$N_s = 400 \cdot \frac{9.0}{120}$$

$$N_s = 400 \cdot 0.075$$

$$N_s = 30$$

**Answer:**
The secondary coil must have **$30$ turns**.
