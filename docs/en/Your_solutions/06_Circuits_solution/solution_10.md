**Given:**

* Charge ($Q$) = $30 \text{ C}$ (Coulombs)
* Time ($t$) = $2 \text{ ms} = 2 \times 10^{-3} \text{ s} = 0.002 \text{ s}$

**Formula:**
Average current ($I$) is defined as the rate of flow of electric charge over time.


$$I = \frac{Q}{t}$$

**Calculation:**


$$I = \frac{30 \text{ C}}{0.002 \text{ s}}$$

$$I = 15,000 \text{ A}$$

**Answer:**
The average current of the lightning bolt is **$15,000 \text{ Amperes}$** (or $15 \text{ kA}$).
