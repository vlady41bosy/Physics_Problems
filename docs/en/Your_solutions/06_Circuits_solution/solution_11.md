Here is the step-by-step solution to find the power and energy:

**Given:**

* Resistance ($R$) = $100\ \Omega$
* Voltage ($V$) = $50\ \text{V}$
* Time ($t$) = $5 \text{ minutes} = 5 \times 60 \text{ seconds} = 300 \text{ s}$

**1. Power Dissipated ($P$)**
The formula relating power, voltage, and resistance is:


$$P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

Substitute the given values into the formula:


$$P = \frac{50^2}{100}$$

$$P = \frac{2500}{100}$$


**$P = 25\ \text{W}$** (Watts)

**2. Energy Consumed ($E$)**
Energy is the product of power and time (in seconds):


$$E = P \cdot t$$

Substitute the values we found:


$$E = 25\ \text{W} \cdot 300\ \text{s}$$


**$E = 7,500\ \text{J}$** (Joules)

**Answer:**

* The power dissipated by the resistor is **$25\ \text{W}$**.
* The energy consumed in 5 minutes is **$7,500\ \text{J}$** (or $7.5\ \text{kJ}$).
