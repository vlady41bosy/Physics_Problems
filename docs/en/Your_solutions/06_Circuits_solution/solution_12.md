Here is the step-by-step solution to find the secondary voltage and primary current:

**Given:**

* Primary turns ($N_p$) = 1000
* Secondary turns ($N_s$) = 200
* Primary voltage ($V_p$) = 120 V
* Secondary current ($I_s$) = 3 A

**1. Secondary Voltage ($V_s$)**
The relationship between voltage and the number of turns in a transformer is given by the formula:


$$\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p}$$

Rearranging to solve for $V_s$:


$$V_s = V_p \cdot \frac{N_s}{N_p}$$

Substitute the given values:


$$V_s = 120 \cdot \frac{200}{1000}$$

$$V_s = 120 \cdot 0.2$$


**$V_s = 24\text{ V}$**

**2. Primary Current ($I_p$)**
Assuming an ideal transformer (where power in equals power out, $P_p = P_s$), the relationship between current and the number of turns is inversely proportional:


$$\frac{I_p}{I_s} = \frac{N_s}{N_p}$$

Rearranging to solve for $I_p$:


$$I_p = I_s \cdot \frac{N_s}{N_p}$$

Substitute the given values:


$$I_p = 3 \cdot \frac{200}{1000}$$

$$I_p = 3 \cdot 0.2$$


**$I_p = 0.6\text{ A}$**

**Answer:**

* The secondary voltage is **24 V**.
* The current in the primary coil is **0.6 A**.
